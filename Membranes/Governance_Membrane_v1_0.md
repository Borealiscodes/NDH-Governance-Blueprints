# 📘 **Governance Membrane (v1.0)**  
### *Governance Altitude • Boundary Layer • NonAuthority Governance*

---

## ⭐ **1. Purpose**  
The Governance Membrane defines the structural boundaries that protect NDH’s governance altitude. It ensures governance artifacts operate within clarity‑aligned, non‑authority constraints and do not contaminate principle, expressive, or diagnostic altitudes.

---

## ⭐ **2. Membrane Identity**  
The governance membrane is:

- altitude‑specific  
- non‑hierarchical  
- clarity‑driven  
- structurally protective  
- lineage‑aware  

It is the **boundary layer** that keeps governance stable.

---

## ⭐ **3. Membrane Boundaries**

Governance altitude **may**:

- apply principles  
- define protocols  
- structure decision flows  
- maintain lineage  
- produce blueprint artifacts  

Governance altitude **may not**:

- define principles  
- generate meta‑structures  
- activate expressive systems  
- perform diagnostic evaluation  
- execute routing geometry  
- override principle altitude  

These boundaries prevent altitude collapse.

---

## ⭐ **4. Membrane Ethics**

The governance membrane enforces:

- non‑authority governance  
- clarity‑aligned decision structures  
- altitude separation  
- blueprint stability  
- safe interaction with principle altitude  
- lineage‑safe evolution  

Membrane ethics ensure NDH remains non‑hierarchical.

---

## ⭐ **5. Interaction Rules**

Governance altitude interacts with:

- **Principle altitude** → through TGL  
- **Expressive altitude** → through membrane filters  
- **Diagnostic altitude** → through governance protocols  
- **Routing geometry** → through decision flow architecture  

All interactions must pass through membrane‑safe channels.

---

## ⭐ **6. Membrane Anchors**

The Governance Membrane anchors:

- Governance Charter  
- Governance Lineage Map  
- Decision Flow Architecture  
- Governance Skeleton  
- Alignment, Coordination, Resilience Protocols  

These artifacts rely on membrane boundaries.

---

## ⭐ **7. Lineage Clause**

The membrane must evolve through:

- explicit versioning  
- documented lineage  
- non‑retroactive changes  
- altitude‑safe updates  

No membrane may be replaced without lineage continuity.

---

## ⭐ **8. Non‑Activation Clause**

This membrane does not activate expressive systems, routing geometry, diagnostic subsystems, or principle engines. It is structural and conceptual.
