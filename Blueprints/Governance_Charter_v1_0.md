# 📘 **NDH Governance Charter (v1.0)**  
### *Governance Altitude • NonAuthority Governance • Blueprint Layer*

---

## ⭐ **1. Purpose**  
The NDH Governance Charter defines the structural commitments, boundaries, and non‑authority governance logic that guide all NDH systems. It ensures clarity, altitude separation, membrane ethics, and stable blueprint evolution.

---

## ⭐ **2. Governance Identity**  
NDH governance is:

- non‑hierarchical  
- clarity‑driven  
- membrane‑safe  
- structurally coherent  
- altitude‑aware  

Governance does **not** imply authority.  
It implies **structure without hierarchy**.

---

## ⭐ **3. Commitments**

- Maintain clarity in all governance artifacts  
- Preserve altitude boundaries between principle, governance, expressive, and diagnostic layers  
- Prevent authority drift, metaphor contamination, and structural collapse  
- Ensure governance decisions follow blueprint logic, not personal interpretation  
- Maintain lineage integrity across versions and expansions  

---

## ⭐ **4. Governance Boundaries**

Governance altitude **does not**:

- define principles  
- generate meta‑structures  
- activate expressive systems  
- perform diagnostic evaluation  
- execute routing geometry  

Governance altitude **does**:

- apply principles  
- define membranes  
- establish protocols  
- maintain lineage  
- structure decision flows  

---

## ⭐ **5. Governance Membrane**

The governance membrane ensures:

- non‑authority governance  
- clarity‑aligned decision structures  
- altitude separation  
- blueprint stability  
- safe interaction with principle altitude  

---

## ⭐ **6. Governance Blueprint Layer**

Blueprint artifacts include:

- Governance Charter  
- Governance Membrane  
- Governance Lineage Map  
- Decision Flow Architecture  
- Governance Skeleton  
- Alignment, Coordination, Resilience Protocols  

These define *how* NDH behaves structurally.

---

## ⭐ **7. Lineage Clause**

All governance artifacts must:

- maintain version clarity  
- preserve structural ancestry  
- avoid retroactive contamination  
- evolve through explicit lineage maps  

---

## ⭐ **8. Non‑Activation Clause**

This Charter does not activate expressive systems, routing geometry, diagnostic subsystems, or principle engines. It is structural and conceptual.
