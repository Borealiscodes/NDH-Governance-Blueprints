# 📘 **Decision Flow Architecture (v1.0)**  
### *Governance Altitude • Operational Layer • Clarity‑Driven Decision Structures*

---

## ⭐ **1. Purpose**  
The Decision Flow Architecture defines how governance decisions are structured, sequenced, and constrained within NDH. It ensures decisions follow clarity‑aligned, non‑authority governance logic and remain membrane‑safe across altitudes.

---

## ⭐ **2. Architecture Identity**  
Decision architecture in NDH is:

- non‑hierarchical  
- clarity‑driven  
- membrane‑aligned  
- altitude‑aware  
- blueprint‑structured  

It governs *how* decisions are made, not *who* makes them.

---

## ⭐ **3. Decision Flow Principles**

NDH governance decisions must:

- follow clarity before action  
- maintain altitude separation  
- respect membrane boundaries  
- preserve lineage continuity  
- avoid authority drift  
- remain structurally coherent  

These principles prevent collapse and contamination.

---

## ⭐ **4. Decision Flow Structure**

NDH decision flows follow a **four‑stage architecture**:

### **Stage 1 — Clarification**
Identify the altitude, membrane, and scope of the decision.

### **Stage 2 — Alignment**
Align the decision with:

- governance charter  
- governance membrane  
- lineage map  
- blueprint constraints  

### **Stage 3 — Structuring**
Apply the correct governance protocol:

- alignment protocol  
- coordination protocol  
- resilience protocol  

### **Stage 4 — Execution**
Execute the decision through membrane‑safe channels.

---

## ⭐ **5. Decision Flow Diagram (Text‑Form)**

```
Clarification
   ↓ identifies altitude + membrane
Alignment
   ↓ aligns with charter + lineage
Structuring
   ↓ applies governance protocols
Execution
   ↓ membrane-safe decision output
```

---

## ⭐ **6. Architecture Boundaries**

Decision Flow Architecture **may**:

- structure governance decisions  
- apply governance protocols  
- enforce membrane ethics  
- maintain altitude separation  

Decision Flow Architecture **may not**:

- define principles  
- activate expressive systems  
- perform diagnostic evaluation  
- generate meta‑structures  
- override lineage maps
